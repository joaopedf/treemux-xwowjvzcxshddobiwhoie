# MedScope AI

**Multi-Agent Clinical Reasoning Platform**

Doctors spend hours cross-referencing symptoms, drug databases, and medical literature for a single patient case — MedScope AI does it in seconds. Built as a multi-agent orchestration system, five specialized AI agents collaboratively parse patient cases from natural language, generate ranked differential diagnoses with ICD-10 codes and confidence levels, create evidence-based treatment plans with dosing and evidence grades, screen for dangerous drug interactions across current and proposed medications, and cite relevant landmark trials and clinical guidelines. The result is presented in a Bloomberg Terminal-inspired clinical dashboard with real-time agent progress visualization, severity-coded alerts, and interactive exploration across every dimension of the clinical analysis. What makes MedScope unique is its agent architecture — each agent is a domain specialist (diagnostician, pharmacologist, pharmacist, literature researcher) that produces structured, auditable output, mirroring how real clinical teams operate. This isn't a chatbot giving general medical advice; it's a precision clinical reasoning engine that could fundamentally change how doctors interact with medical knowledge at the point of care.
